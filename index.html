Fair-play terms Read before rated play

Rated results should reflect the moves played on the board.

  • No engine assistance in rated games.
  • No collusion or sandbagging.
  • Keep play respectful, including chat and invites.
  • Protect clock integrity. Do not pause, alter, or falsify a clock.
FAIR PLAY

Play honestly. Keep the board meaningful.

Nova Chess rated games are designed to reflect the moves made by the people at the board. These terms apply before account creation and rated play.

By using rated games, you agree to:

  • Play without engine assistance or outside move recommendations.
  • Avoid collusion, sandbagging, intentional clock abuse, and falsified results.
  • Keep friend invites and game interactions respectful.
  • Protect the integrity of the server-authoritative clock.

PRIVACY

A small account for a focused chess room.

Nova Chess uses only the information needed to provide accounts, friendships, games, sessions, and ratings.

  • Your handle and display name identify you on the board.
  • Passwords are stored as salted hashes, never as readable passwords.
  • Sessions, friendships, and ratings are stored in the server database.
  • Active game state is kept in memory while a server process is running.
  • No email address, advertising, tracking, or browser API keys are requested.
  • Password recovery is unavailable, so keep your password safe.
